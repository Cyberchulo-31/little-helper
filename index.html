Where to get his school books

All Greek school books are free from the Ministry of Education's official e-book library.

  1. Open ebooks.edu.gr → Α΄ Γυμνασίου (opens in a new tab).
  2. Pick the subject, then download the PDF of the student book (Βιβλίο Μαθητή) or workbook (Τετράδιο Εργασιών).
  3. Come back here and press Add a PDF book.

Some schools now use a different book from the approved list (πολλαπλό βιβλίο). If his book isn't on the site, take photos of the pages instead.

Tip: press ⌘V any time to add another screenshot to this book.

Your quests

Each question is one step. Solve them one at a time.

How to play

  1. Paste your homework (or snap a photo). Buddy splits it into questions.
  2. Press Guide me on a question.
  3. Buddy explains what it's asking and gives you one small step at a time. You do the work.
  4. Type your answer. Buddy checks it. Solve it to earn XP!

+10 XP per question · +50 XP per quest · +25 XP per focus run

Level up faster

  • Try each step yourself before asking for a hint.
  • Use Focus mode with a short timer.
  • Come back every day to grow your streak.

      How Quest Day works

      • Every day a new boss shows up, built from the homework you did yesterday.
      • Each right answer hits the boss. You have 3 hearts. A wrong answer costs a heart, and you get a hint to try again.
      • Beat the boss before your hearts run out to win a Mystery Drop and your prize!
